LEARN WITHOUT THE JARGON

Health library.

Simple educational guides to help you understand common organs and laboratory tests.

◉
ORGAN GUIDE

Liver function

The liver helps process nutrients, break down substances and make proteins. Tests such as ALT and AST can provide information about liver cells.

ALT · AST · Bilirubin
◒
ORGAN GUIDE

Kidney function

The kidneys filter waste and extra fluid from blood. Creatinine and eGFR are among the measures used to assess kidney function.

Creatinine · Urea · eGFR
♡
ORGAN GUIDE

Heart & lipids

Lipid tests measure different fats in blood. Their interpretation depends on the full health context and a clinician's assessment.

LDL · HDL · Triglycerides
✣
TEST GUIDE

Complete blood count

A CBC measures components of blood, including red cells, white cells and platelets. It is interpreted alongside symptoms and other findings.

Hemoglobin · WBC · Platelets
ⓘ

These guides are general education only. They do not interpret your personal results or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
